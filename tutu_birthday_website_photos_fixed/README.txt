TUTU'S BIRTHDAY WEBSITE

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the website in Chrome.

IMPORTANT
- Keep the assets folder beside index.html. Do not move index.html out of the folder.
- The photos are local files in assets/. They do not require an internet connection.
- Camera/microphone candle blowing requires HTTPS and browser permission. The manual candle button works locally too.
